Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

ITM Publisher is a private tool used only by Into The Machine to upload and schedule our own videos on our own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page.

What we access

With our own authorization, the tool uses the YouTube Data API and the Meta Graph API to upload videos, set schedules and read basic details of our own channels, such as channel name and post status.

Other people's data

The tool is not offered to the public. It does not collect, sell or share data about other people.

Storage

Access tokens are stored privately on our own computer and are never shared. They can be removed at any time.

YouTube and Google

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked in Google account permissions.

Contact and deletion

Questions or deletion requests: intothemachineai@gmail.com
